# Vibbr

Find what's on. Go together. Events and tickets on Oxy: publish an event, sell or hand out tickets
(free, pay at the door, or online), pass a ticket to a friend or resell it at a capped price, and
scan people in at the door, offline included.

- API: Express 5 + Drizzle on PostgreSQL. App: Expo (iOS, Android, web) with Bloom.
- Sign-in, accounts and organizations are Oxy's; nothing about a person is copied here.
- Mention shares an event by its id through `@vibbr.co/sdk`.

See [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md) for commands and conventions and [docs/](./docs) for the decisions.
